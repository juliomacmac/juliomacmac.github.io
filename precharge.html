Voltage comparison and relay sequencing for precharge.

My contribution

I reviewed and contributed redesign work to the precharge board, including its integration with the vehicle electrical system.

Design

The design compares the DC bus voltage with a reference derived from the battery voltage. It uses relay control and delay circuitry to sequence the precharge path and its bypass.
