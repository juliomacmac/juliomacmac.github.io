ShieldESP display

An ESP32-based board for the vehicle display.

My contribution

I was a main contributor to the display project, working on the ShieldESP hardware and developing the display’s user interface.

Design

ShieldESP connects an ESP32 module to the display and the vehicle CAN bus. Separate schematic sheets cover the controller, power supply, and connectors.

The images on this page show the PCB. They are not screenshots of the display interface.
