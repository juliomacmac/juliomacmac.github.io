T11 · EESC-USP Tupã

Power Distribution Module

Electronic power distribution for the Formula SAE vehicle.

My contribution

I designed the schematic and PCB, and guided the firmware logic. Another team member implemented the firmware. The board was manufactured, tested, and installed in the vehicle.

Design

The PDM brings high-side switching, current-measurement circuitry, and CAN communication onto a four-layer board. Its copper stack is SIG1 / GND / PWR / SIG2, with the ground and power layers between the outer signal layers.
